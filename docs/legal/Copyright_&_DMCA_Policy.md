## 3. Submitting a DMCA Notice of Infringement

If you believe that your copyrighted work (e.g., food photography, restaurant menu graphic, proprietary logo) has been uploaded or displayed on `kafei.in` without your authorization, please send a written notification to our Designated Copyright Agent containing the following statutory information:

1. **Physical or Electronic Signature**: An authorized signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on their behalf.
2. **Identification of the Copyrighted Work**: A clear description of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or a representative list if multiple works are involved.
3. **Identification of the Infringing Material**: The specific URL(s) or exact location on `kafei.in` where the allegedly infringing material is located (e.g., restaurant menu link or image asset URL).
4. **Contact Information**: Your full legal name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
5. **Good Faith Statement**: A statement that you have a good faith belief that the disputed use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
6. **Accuracy & Penalty of Perjury Statement**: A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

## 4. Takedown & Counter-Notification Procedure

### 4.1 Notice Processing & Takedown
Upon receipt of a valid, compliant DMCA notice, Kafei will promptly remove or disable access to the infringing material and notify the affected restaurant tenant who uploaded the content.

### 4.2 Counter-Notification
If a restaurant tenant believes their material was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, they may file a written Counter-Notification with our Designated Agent containing:
1. The tenant's physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the material that was removed and the location where it appeared before removal.
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that the tenant has a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
4. The tenant's name, address, telephone number, email address, and consent to the jurisdiction of the competent courts in Kolkata, West Bengal, India.

If a valid counter-notice is received, Kafei may restore the disabled content after 10 to 14 business days unless the original claimant files a court action seeking a restraining order.
