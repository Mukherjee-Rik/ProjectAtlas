## 1. Overview

This Refund and Cancellation Policy outlines the terms governing subscription cancellations, trial periods, and refund eligibility for our restaurant management software.

## 2. 14-Day Free Trial (No Card Required)

- Every new restaurant workspace begins with a **14-day full-access free trial**.
- You do **not** need to provide a credit card or payment instrument to start your trial.
- You will never be charged automatically upon trial expiration. If you choose not to subscribe, your workspace transitions to inactive status without any financial obligation.

## 3. Subscription Cancellation Policy

### 3.1 Self-Service Cancellation
You may cancel your Kafei subscription at any time without talking to a salesperson:
1. Sign in to your Kafei account at [https://kafei.in/login](https://kafei.in/login).
2. Navigate to `Dashboard > Settings > Billing & Plans`.
3. Click **Cancel Subscription** and confirm.

### 3.2 Effective Cancellation Date
- Upon cancellation, your subscription remains active until the end of your currently paid billing period (month or year).
- You will retain uninterrupted access to your dining floor, KDS screens, and cashier POS until the current period expires.
- No further renewal charges will be billed to your payment method.

---

## 4. Refund Eligibility & Terms

### 4.1 Annual Subscriptions (30-Day Money-Back Guarantee)
- **Within 30 Days of Purchase**: If you purchase an annual subscription and are unsatisfied for any reason, you may request a **100% full refund** within thirty (30) days of the initial subscription date.
- **After 30 Days**: Annual subscriptions canceled after 30 days are non-refundable, but access remains active through the end of the annual term.

### 4.2 Monthly Subscriptions
- Monthly subscriptions are billed in advance for each 30-day cycle and are generally non-refundable once the cycle has commenced.
- **Service Outage Exceptions**: If your restaurant experiences a verified, continuous system outage directly caused by Kafei's cloud infrastructure exceeding our 99.9% uptime SLA, you may request a pro-rata service credit or refund for the affected period.

### 4.3 Hardware & Thermal Printers
- Kafei is a pure cloud-based web application that operates on standard commodity hardware (tablets, PCs, 80mm ESC/POS thermal printers).
- Kafei does not manufacture or sell proprietary physical hardware. Returns and refunds for third-party thermal printers or POS stands must be handled directly with your hardware vendor.
