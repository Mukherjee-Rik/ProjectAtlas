## 1. Purpose & Scope

At **Kafei**, we leverage modern Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) technologies to empower restaurant operators with natural language sales insights, automated demand forecasting, kitchen preparation pacing, and inventory optimization.

This AI Usage and Responsible Use Policy outlines the principles, safeguards, data privacy standards, and human-in-the-loop governance applied across all AI-driven features in the Kafei platform at **[https://kafei.in](https://kafei.in)**.

---

## 2. AI Capabilities in Kafei

Kafei incorporates AI to deliver the following assistive functionalities:
1. **AI Restaurant Copilot**: Enables operators to ask natural language questions regarding their restaurant data (e.g., *"Which dish had the highest margin this weekend?"*, *"What were our peak dining hours on Friday?"*).
2. **Predictive Demand Forecasting**: Evaluates historical table turnover, seasonal trends, and item popularity to help kitchens prep appropriate ingredient quantities and minimize food waste.
3. **Automated Revenue & Shift Summaries**: Synthesizes end-of-day sales, category breakdowns, and void request metrics into executive summaries.
4. **Smart Menu Optimization Suggestions**: Recommends item pairing suggestions and combo bundles based on customer ordering patterns.

## 3. Responsible AI Principles & Commitments

### 3.1 Strict Privacy & Zero Model Training on Customer Data
- **No Training on Private Restaurant Data**: Your restaurant sales data, secret recipes, guest orders, profit margins, and proprietary business metrics are **never** used to train, retrain, or improve generalized or public AI foundation models.
- **No Training on Google OAuth Data**: Personal data obtained via Google Sign-In (names, emails, avatars) is strictly excluded from AI prompt context and is never provided to model training pipelines.
- **Enterprise Zero-Data-Retention Guarantees**: When communicating with external AI inference APIs (e.g., Google Cloud Vertex AI / Gemini Enterprise API), all requests are processed under enterprise commercial terms with zero data retention for model training.

### 3.2 Human-in-the-Loop Governance
- **Assistive, Not Autonomous Decision-Making**: AI recommendations (e.g., menu price adjustments, inventory purchase orders, staff schedule suggestions) are advisory only.
- **Human Confirmation Required**: No commercial transaction, price change, void approval, or billing deduction is executed autonomously by an AI agent without explicit human operator confirmation.

### 3.3 Transparency & Clear Labeling
- All AI-generated suggestions, automated forecasts, and copilot responses within the Kafei interface are visibly badged with an AI indicator (✨) so users always know when output was generated by machine intelligence.

### 3.4 Fairness, Safety & Non-Discrimination
- Our AI models are tested against bias and do not incorporate discriminatory heuristics based on race, gender, nationality, age, religion, or protected characteristics.

## 4. Limitations & Accuracy Caveats

While Kafei's AI features strive for high precision:
1. **Statistical Nature**: AI outputs are probabilistic estimations based on historical trend lines.
2. **Operator Verification**: Restaurant owners and accounting personnel must verify all automated tax figures, financial summaries, and invoice totals against raw transaction logs prior to statutory tax filings.
3. **No Professional Legal or Tax Advice**: AI-generated responses do not constitute formal legal, accounting, tax, or health safety counsel.

---

## 5. User Controls & Opt-Out

Restaurant administrators have full control over AI features:
- You may disable the AI Copilot and automated predictive forecasting at any time via `Dashboard > Settings > AI & Automations > Disable AI Assist`.
- Disabling AI features does not impact standard floor operations, table QR ordering, KDS ticket routing, or POS cashier settlement.
