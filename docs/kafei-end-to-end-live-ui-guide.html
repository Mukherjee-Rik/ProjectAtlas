How Kafei Powers a Real Dinner Service

This document demonstrates the live user interface (UI) and technical mechanics of Kafei 2.0. See exactly what your customers, kitchen staff, waiters, and cashiers experience during peak rush hours.

Stage 1: At The Table Guest Experience

1. Guest Scans Table Standee & Orders Without App Download

When customers sit at Table 04, they scan the acrylic table QR code using their iPhone or Android camera. The menu opens immediately in their default browser with high-resolution dish photos, modifiers, and spice preferences.

  • Zero Customer Resistance: Guests do not download any app or enter their phone number to see the menu.
  • Live Cooking Countdown: As soon as the kitchen accepts the ticket, the guest's phone shows a real-time countdown timer.
  • Multi-Round Ordering: If guests order a second round of drinks 40 minutes later, it joins the same active table session automatically.

Stage 3: On The Floor Waiter Terminal

3. Floor Staff Manage Tables on Their Own Smartphones

Waiters carry standard Android or iOS phones. When the kitchen marks food as "Ready", the waiter's terminal receives an instant chime. Waitstaff can also punch in verbal orders, modify seats, or request voids.

Stage 4: At The Cashier Counter Settlement & Audit

4. Instant 80mm GST Thermal Receipt & Anti-Theft Security

When guests settle their bill, the cashier screen handles cash, card, UPI QR, and split bills. Receipts print directly from the browser without third-party drivers.

  • Anti-Theft Manager Queue: Waiters cannot cancel dishes on their own. All item cancellations, bill voids, and discounts require an explanatory reason and 1-click manager approval.
  • Recipe-Level Inventory Deduction: Ingredients are deducted automatically from stock in real-time as dishes are cooked.
  • Multi-Branch Cloud Dashboard: Real-time consolidated sales, gross margins, and tax breakdowns update live on the owner's dashboard.
