1 Restaurant Owner: Setup, Menu & Dashboard

Everything you need to launch and monitor your restaurant in 6 fast steps.

1

Sign Up & Restaurant Profile (`/onboarding`)

Register your account with your restaurant name and email. Choose your dining concept (e.g. Fine Dining, Cafe) and verify currency (INR ₹).

What You See: Guided 6-step progress bar showing 0% to 100% setup completion.
2

Setup Floor Dining Areas & Table QR Codes (`/tables`)

Define your dining sections (e.g. Courtyard, AC Hall, Rooftop) and set table count. Atlas automatically generates encrypted QR codes for each table.

What You See: Downloadable QR sticker designs ready for printing on table stands.
3

Menu Engineering & Dish Management (`/menus`)

Create categories (Starters, Biryanis, Drinks), add dish photos, set prices, and dietary flags (Veg 🟢, Non-Veg 🔴). Toggle "Out of Stock" with 1 click!

What You See: Clean visual card grid with instant live sync to all diner phones.
4

Live Operations Dashboard & AI Copilot (`/dashboard`)

Monitor real-time sales revenue, occupied tables, and peak dining hours. Ask the AI Copilot natural questions: "Which dish made the most profit today?"

What You See: Live revenue stats, occupancy meters, hourly heatmaps, and AI chat.

Frictionless digital ordering directly inside mobile web browsers.

1

Scan QR Sticker on Table

Customer opens phone camera, scans the table code, and the live interactive menu opens instantly without downloading an app or registering.

2

Select Dishes & Shared Table Cart

Diners add dishes, choose portion sizes, specify chef notes (e.g. "Extra sauce, no nuts"), and tap "Submit Table Order".

3

Live Prep Tracking & Table Service Calls

The screen shows live progress (Pending ➔ Cooking ➔ Ready). Diners can tap "Call Waiter" or "Water Refill" at any moment.

Order Arrives (Audio Alert)

Chime rings and the new ticket appears under "New Orders" with table number, quantities, and highlighted chef notes.

2

Start Cooking ➔ Ready for Pickup

Cook taps "Start Cooking" (timer starts). When plated, cook taps "Ready for Pickup". Floor waitstaff are notified immediately!

4 Floor Waitstaff & Cashier Billing

🛎️

Floor Waiter Flow (`/waiter`)

• Food Delivery: See pulsing green alerts when kitchen finishes cooking. Pick up hot food and tap "Mark Served".
• Table Calls: Real-time badges for customer requests (Water, Waiter, Bill).
• Manual Ordering: Take orders for walk-ins on mobile phone and send directly to kitchen.

💵

Cashier & POS Settlement (`/cashier`)

• Review Bill: Select table, review ordered items, and verify automatic taxes (CGST/SGST).
• Apply Discounts: Enter promo codes or percentage discounts.
• Accept Payment: Collect via Cash (with change calculator), Dynamic UPI QR, or Card POS machine.
• Close Table: Generates printed receipt and automatically frees up the table for the next diners!

5 Platform Admin Flow (`/platform-admin`)

Central management desk for Atlas operators and engineers.

🛡️

Global Network & Support Incident Desk

• Platform Overview: Total active restaurants, network revenue (MRR), and system health.
• Customer Support Desk: Manage tickets submitted by restaurant owners (e.g. ATLAS-0FB5). Type resolution notes and resolve incidents.
• Subscription Controls: Upgrade restaurants between Free, Growth, and Multi-Branch Enterprise tiers.

6 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Can customers order if they don't have mobile internet?
Yes! The waiter can take their order directly on the handheld waiter screen (`/waiter`).
Q: How fast do menu price changes update?
Instantly! When you edit a price in `/menus`, all customer phone screens update immediately without reloading.
Q: How do I get help if my kitchen printer stops working?
Click "Support" in your sidebar and click "+ Create Ticket". Atlas engineers receive your ticket with priority SLA!
