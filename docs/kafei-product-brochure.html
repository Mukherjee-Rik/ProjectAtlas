🔄 How Kafei Works During a Live Dinner Service

From the moment a guest sits down until the bill is settled, the workflow is automated.

STEP 01

Scan & Browse

Guest scans the table QR. The visual menu loads instantly with dietary tags and dish photos.

STEP 02

Instant KOT Route

Order fires directly to the Kitchen KDS. Chef taps "Start Cooking" and the guest sees a live timer.

STEP 03

Plated & Served

Chef bumps the order to "Ready". Waiter terminal alerts floor staff to serve hot dishes.

STEP 04

Settle & Clear

Guest pays via UPI QR or counter. Cashier prints 80mm GST receipt; table resets instantly.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up Kafei.

Do I need to buy expensive POS computers?

No! Kafei runs on any device with a browser: iPads, Android tablets, laptops, counter PCs, and staff smartphones. You save ₹50,000+ in upfront hardware costs.

Can I print 80mm GST thermal receipts?

Yes. Kafei generates standard 80mm and 58mm thermal receipts with full itemized GST breakdown, restaurant branding, and table tokens directly to any USB/Bluetooth/LAN printer.

How does the UPI QR payment work?

Upload your UPI QR in Settings. When customers view the bill on their phone, your QR is displayed with the exact total for 0% commission direct bank settlement.

Can I manage multiple dining areas or branches?

Yes. You can manage indoor, patio, bar, and rooftop areas, as well as multiple restaurant outlets under a single unified owner login.
