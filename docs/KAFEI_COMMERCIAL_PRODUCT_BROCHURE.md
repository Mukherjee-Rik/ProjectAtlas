### Screen 1: The Guest Table (App-Free QR Standee)
* **Zero App Download**: Guests scan the table acrylic standee with their native phone camera.
* **Visual Menu & Customization**: High-resolution dish photos, dietary badges (Veg/Non-Veg/Vegan), portion sizes, and cooking notes.
* **Live Cooking Timer**: Once the kitchen accepts the ticket, a live countdown shows when food is arriving.
* **0% Commission UPI Checkout**: Guests view the bill total alongside your restaurant’s UPI QR code.

### Screen 2: The Kitchen Display System (KDS)
* **Paperless Digital KOT**: Eliminates greasy lost tickets and printer jams.
* **Color-Coded Timers**: Tickets shift from Green (Fresh) &rarr; Yellow (In-progress) &rarr; Red (Delayed).
* **Allergen & Modifier Highlights**: Special requests ("No onions", "Extra cheese") appear in bold amber callouts.
* **Bump Bar Completion**: 1-tap marks dishes ready, automatically alerting floor staff.

### Screen 3: The Waiter Mobile Handheld Terminal
* **Staff Phone Compatibility**: Waiters carry their own smartphones or light Android tablets.
* **Table-Side Ordering**: Take verbal orders, modify active tables, and transfer tables.
* **Real-Time Floor Sync**: Clears table state the moment billing settles so tables can be reseated immediately.

### Screen 4: The Cashier POS & Owner Command Center
* **Ultra-Fast Thermal Billing**: Prints 80mm & 58mm GST-compliant thermal receipts directly from the web browser.
* **Multi-Mode Settlement**: Seamlessly supports UPI QR, Cash, Card, and split billing across guests.
* **Anti-Theft Manager Audit Trail**: Waiters cannot quietly cancel orders. All voids and refunds require an explanatory reason and 1-click manager approval.
* **Recipe-Level Inventory Tracking**: Ingredients deduct as dishes leave the kitchen pass, not at midnight.

## 4. Step-by-Step Dinner Service Flow

1. **Step 1: Scan & Browse**: Guest sits down at Table 04 and scans the QR standee. The menu opens with photos.
2. **Step 2: Instant Kitchen Routing**: Guest hits "Order". Ticket #AT-0041 flashes on the Kitchen KDS screen. Chef taps "Start Cooking".
3. **Step 3: Plated & Served**: Chef finishes plating and taps "Ready". Waiter terminal receives a chime to serve hot food.
4. **Step 4: 0% Commission Checkout**: Guest views bill on phone or walks to counter. Cashier prints 80mm thermal receipt with GST breakdown. Table resets for next guest.
